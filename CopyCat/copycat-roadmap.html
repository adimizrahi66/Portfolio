Scoring
Score each item across 6 dimensions. Priority = weighted average.
Score formula
Priority Score = (Impact × 1.8 + Reach × 1.4 + Severity × 1.4 + Strategic Fit × 1.2) ÷ 4 − (Effort × 0.6) + Dependency Boost
Scores are normalized to 1–10. Effort is a cost — higher effort lowers the score. Dependencies flag blockers.
