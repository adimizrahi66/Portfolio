About Me

Hey, I'm Adi

Background

My Story

Design and art have been a constant thread in my life - from early childhood sketches, through a bachelor's degree in design, into every role I've taken since. Making things with my hands and watching an idea become real has always been where I do my best thinking.

Alongside the craft, I've always been fascinated by psychology - why people behave the way they do, what drives a decision, where attention goes and why. The moment I realized product design sits exactly at the intersection of those two worlds, the path was obvious: UX wasn't just a career choice - it was the alignment of everything I care about.

That intersection still shapes how I work today. I dive deep into research to uncover real user insight, treat data as the sharpest design tool I have, and hold my work to a double standard: it has to be visually precise and resonate with the people using it. I learn fast, adapt faster, and I'm at my best when the problem is messy and the bar is high.

📊

Evidence over opinion

Every design decision starts with research and ends with measurement. If it can't be validated, it's a guess - and I don't ship guesses.

🧠

Psychology-first UX

Craft under constraints

Real products live between user needs, business goals, and technical limits. I design for that reality - at pace, without cutting corners.
