Context

Scaling creative production for Meta acquisition

At Roundforest, the Meta acquisition team operates in the upper funnel. Performance at this stage depends on scale – producing large volumes of ad creatives and running continuous tests. Creative production is a daily operation, and increasing output directly impacts growth and profitability.

The Challenge

The core challenge

Creative production was fully manual and couldn’t keep up with the scale the funnel demanded.

Creative production was fully manual, capped at 70–100 creatives per day.
To reach breakeven, we needed at least 300 creatives daily.
A no-code system was later introduced, increasing scale by 30% – but efficiency remained a bottleneck.

The Problem

A faster process that became harder to run

The new system lacked context and wasn’t integrated with the team’s workflow.
To compensate, the team stitched together ~6 different tools, creating a fragmented and manual-heavy process.
While production became faster, it became significantly more complex and inefficient.

UX Strategy

A platform, not just a generator

I approached the system as a holistic platform, not just a creative generator. Three principles guided every decision:

Unify →

Bring all tools into one system, replacing six disconnected steps with a single continuous flow.

Automate →

Automate everything possible – data handling, layout, and asset generation – to remove manual work.

Review →

Keep human review where it matters, protecting quality on high-sensitivity decisions.

Solution

One task list that drives the whole flow

The system is built around a task & content list – the starting point of the daily workflow. Each task includes article (landing page) generation and creative generation. The system pulls all relevant data directly from each task, enabling a seamless flow from idea to live campaign.

The task list – a single source of truth that triggers article & creative generation

Key decisions

Single source of truth: one list drives the entire process.
End-to-end flow: from content → creatives → campaign → testing.
Step-based creation flow: reduces errors, ensures completeness, and enables structured automation.
Built-in review points: maintains quality without slowing down scale.
